FORK submission demo
Narration: offline macOS Samantha system voice, not the owner's voice.
Actual local UI; synthetic data; Deterministic Planner Mode.

01 · 0s–10s · A decision before dispatch
Which plan still feeds people when a vehicle fails? FORK lets a coordinator test that decision before dispatch, with a synthetic mission.

02 · 10s–22s · One shared starting point
This food bank has two hundred forty meals, two hundred sixty four requested, and three vehicles. Each strategy receives an isolated copy of the same resources and constraints.

03 · 22s–38s · Keep the baseline honest
Velocity, Resilience, and Equity express different priorities in a deterministic planner. Ordinary greedy routing delivers two hundred twenty four meals initially, beating every named policy. FORK keeps that loss visible.

04 · 38s–51s · Break the same vehicle
Now vehicle A fails at minute twenty five in every world. Completed deliveries stay completed. Its cargo stays stranded. Recovery can change only the remaining plan.

05 · 51s–68s · Measure the recovery
All three adapted plans now deliver one hundred sixty eight meals. With fixed routes under the same failure, they deliver one hundred forty four, one hundred twenty, and one hundred forty four. This measures recovery against a paired control.

06 · 68s–81s · Make priorities explicit
Better depends on the coordinator's priorities. Adjust the weights for coverage, high need service, and modeled cost. The comparison uses verified outcomes and reports ties instead of inventing a winner.

07 · 81s–96s · Open the actual receipt
A separate referee reconstructs movement, cargo, and timing, then recomputes the metrics. Its receipt checks consistency inside the declared model. It does not prove route optimality or real world safety.

08 · 96s–115s · A person makes the call
A person reviews the evidence and approves this revision. The exported packet carries the simulated plan and its verification. Nothing is dispatched. FORK makes the tradeoffs inspectable, and leaves the final decision with the coordinator.
